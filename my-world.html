Genre: cozy town builder. Platform: mobile (Unity). Status: in development. The design document and the art kit exist; the game is not playable yet.

You place zones and landmarks, and the town develops a character of its own: rural or urban, mystical or industrial, open or fortified. Seasons change how it looks and how it plays.

Art kit

I build the town from modular pieces. Every piece shares one material and one texture sheet, which keeps draw calls low on a phone. It is also how I taught myself UV mapping.

Town identity

A town's identity is tracked on five axes, each with two opposite poles. The player sees it as a pentagon. Building more of one thing pushes the town that way, and each step costs resources to lock in.

Seasons

Four seasons, changing every 48 real hours, or locked to the one you like. Spring brings flowers, autumn turns the trees orange, and winter brings snow on the roofs, bare trees and shorter days. Each season is a set of pieces that swap in on the same model.
